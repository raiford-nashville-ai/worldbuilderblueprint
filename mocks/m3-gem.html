The Spark · Primary Inception
01 · Origin Captured

What's its story?

A character might inherit a locked notebook. A venture starts from an impossible trade. A fictional world might begin the night a star refused to rise. Where did your idea first draw breath?
02 · Essence Captured

What makes it matter?

Strip away the surface details. If this didn't exist, what silence, grief, or blank page would remain in the room?
03 · Belief Captured

What does it hold to be true?

What assumption does it treat as non-negotiable scripture? An ethic, an unbendable standard, or an aesthetic truth everyone else dismissed.
04 · Perception Captured

What does it notice that others miss?

Where other systems move too fast to register detail, what subtle fracture or human need does your world keep its eye upon?
05 · Friction Captured

What's holding it back?

Every true manuscript holds an inner paradox. That resistance is not a flaw in your design—it is the friction that gives the story its dramatic weight.
06 · Gravity Captured

What holds everything together?

If wind and chaos strike, what anchor keeps the characters and chapters from spinning apart? A vow? An heirloom? A single room?
07 · Coherence Captured

What makes it stay itself?

As the manuscript grows into volumes or products, what border must never be compromised? What makes it unmistakably itself?
