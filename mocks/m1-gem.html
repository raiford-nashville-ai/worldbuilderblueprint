The Spark
01 · Origin Captured

What's its story?

A character might carry an inherited debt. A business might begin from a persistent frustration. A world might emerge from an ancient forgotten rupture. Where did your idea first draw breath?
02 · Essence Captured

What makes it matter?

Strip away the surface details. If this didn't exist, what silence, injustice, or emptiness would remain? What is the transformation it creates when it enters a room?
03 · Belief Captured

What does it hold to be true?

What assumption does it take as absolute gospel? This could be a founding moral stance, an aesthetic standard, or a premise that everyone else thinks is impossible.
04 · Perception Captured

What does it notice that others miss?

Where others see routine, it sees an opening. Where others see trash, it sees raw power. What detail does it tune into that the conventional world walks straight past?
05 · Friction Captured

What's holding it back?

Every meaningful concept holds an internal contradiction or an adversary. What price does it refuse to pay? What fear or structural obstacle gives it its dramatic weight?
06 · Gravity Captured

What holds everything together?

If chaos strikes, what single pole keeps the parts from spinning into the void? An unbending loyalty? A recurring ritual? An unbreakable physical or emotional law?
07 · Coherence Captured

What makes it stay itself?

Even as it grows, mutates, or scales across years, what must never change? What would compromise it so fundamentally that it would cease to be what you imagined?
