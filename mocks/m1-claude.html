    World Builder Blueprint

    One question, seven more, and a Blueprint built from your own ideas.

    Describe what you want to create — a character, a story, a business, a world, or something you can’t quite explain yet. Seven optional questions help you explore its foundations. Then your answers are gathered into a Blueprint you can read, copy, and keep.

    Your words

    What you write is saved only in this browser, so you don’t lose it if you step away. In this preview, Blueprints are composed on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

    Voice input uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition, which may process audio through the browser maker’s service. The microphone is only on while it says “Listening.”

    Speak instead of typing

    Your words will appear in the field, where you can review and edit them.

    Voice input uses your browser’s speech recognition. Depending on your browser, audio may be processed by its speech service (for example, Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari) to turn it into text. World Builder Blueprint doesn’t record or store audio.

    The microphone stays on only while the button shows it’s listening. Select it again, or press Esc, to stop.
