For Sadie's website, I'd rank these based on brand memorability, emotional appeal, search friendliness, and alignment with the simple “What do you want to create?” experience.

1. myworldblueprint.com — Best overall. Personal, memorable, and broad enough for books, businesses, characters, and any other creation.
2. worldbuilderblueprint.com — Excellent alignment with the framework and strong descriptive keywords. Slightly longer, but highly relevant.
3. createyourworlds.com — Inviting, imaginative, and action-oriented. Particularly good for Sadie's personality and mission.
4. storyworldblueprint.com — Strong for fiction authors and book series, but narrower than the website's universal purpose.
5. createyourbookseries.com — Very clear and search-friendly for aspiring authors, but limits the perceived use cases.
6. worldbuilderbooks.com — Good publishing association, but sounds more like a bookstore or publishing imprint than a universal creation tool.
7. mybookcreation.com — Simple and approachable, but generic and primarily associated with book production.

My recommendation: myworldblueprint.com.

It makes the visitor the center of the experience. The name feels like something they own, not software they have to learn.

And unlike the book-specific domains, it leaves room for Sadie's company to grow without needing to rebrand.

One important distinction: keywords in a domain provide little direct SEO advantage today. Relevant content, search intent, site quality, and brand recognition will matter much more.
